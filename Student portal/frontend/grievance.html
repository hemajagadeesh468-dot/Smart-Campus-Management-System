02 / QUICK ANSWERS

Frequently asked questions

Some answers before you submit.

What happens after I submit a grievance?

Your concern can be reviewed by the relevant department. Once backend integration is active, you will be able to track its status.

Can I report an academic issue?

Yes. Select Academic Issues and describe the problem clearly, including relevant dates if needed.

How can I check my ticket status?

Visit the All Tickets page to view the ticket tracking interface. Live tracking will be connected to the backend later.

What if my concern is urgent?

Select High priority and provide clear details. For immediate safety or emergencies, contact campus authorities directly.
